📍 About DestNotify


Our Mission

Ever missed your stop because you were lost in your phone, jamming to tunes, or snoozing? DestNotify’s here to save the day with timely alerts so you never overshoot again! 🚀 We’re all about making your travels smarter, smoother, and way more fun!

Problems We Faced

  • 📱 Distractions leading to missed stops 😣
  • 🎧 No audible cues over music 😫
  • 💤 Oversleeping past destinations 😴
  • 🍽️ Craving local eats but no ideas 😋
  • 🌧️ Weather surprises ruining plans 😰

How DestNotify Solves This

  • 🔔 Smart Alerts - GPS-powered pings before your stop, with custom proximity settings (10m to infinity!). Never miss your spot again! 🌍
  • 📳 Vibration Alerts - Feel the buzz when you’re near your stop, even with your phone in your pocket or music blasting! 🚀
  • 🪗 Custom Alarms - Pick from 9 funky sounds to wake you up, loud enough to cut through your jams! 🎶
  • ⭐ Favorites - Save your go-to spots (home, work, café) with a tap—or let auto-learning do the magic! ✨
  • 📡 Real-Time Tracking - Pinpoint GPS keeps you locked on, with live distance updates to your destinations! 🗺️
  • 🧠 Auto-Learning - Frequent haunts get added to favorites automatically—no effort, all smarts! 🧠
  • 🌤️ Weather Updates - Live forecasts with travel tips (e.g., “Bring an umbrella!”) for your spots—stay prepared! ☀️🌧️
  • 🍛 Famous Foods - Discover the top 5 local dishes near you or your favorites with one click—foodie heaven awaits! 😋
  • 🤖 DestiNotiX Bot - Chat about destinations, food, weather, or random fun with our AI buddy—your travel BFF! 💬
  • 📩 Email Alerts - Get notified via email when you arrive, coords included—share your journey! 📧
  • 🎉 Party Mode - Celebrate arrivals with emoji explosions and vibes—because you deserve it! 🥳✨
  • 📲 PWA Ready - Install it as an app for offline access and seamless use—travel anywhere, anytime! 📱
  • 💾 Smart Caching - Stores your location data for faster tracking, even with spotty internet—stay on course! 🌐
  • 📰 Local News - Stay in the loop with breaking news near your location or destinations—know what’s buzzing! 🌐
  • 🎬 Movie Mood - Get movie picks based on the weather—rainy day rom-coms or sunny adventure flicks, anyone? 🍿
  • 🎉 Holidays - Check upcoming holidays near you—plan your next getaway or just vibe with the festivities! 🗓️
